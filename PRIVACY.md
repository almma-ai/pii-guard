# Almma’s PII Guard privacy policy

_Last updated: November 28, 2026_

Almma’s PII Guard (PII Guard) is a browser extension published by Almma. It checks text you paste or type into the message box on claude.ai, chatgpt.com, gemini.google.com, copilot.cloud.microsoft and chat.almma.ai for personal data, and offers to replace that data with placeholders before you send it.

## What PII Guard reads

- Text you paste, drop or type into the message box, or into another text box, on claude.ai, chatgpt.com, gemini.google.com, copilot.cloud.microsoft and chat.almma.ai.
- Text you select on those sites when you use its right-click menu.

It does not run on any other website.

## Kinds of data it handles (on your device only)

Because it reads what you write in the chat box, PII Guard handles:

- **Personally identifiable information**: names, e-mail addresses, phone and ID numbers, dates, street addresses and similar details it looks for.
- **Health information**: any medical details in the text you write (for example record numbers, dates of care or other notes) are read in order to check them.
- **Personal communications**: the chat message you are writing.
- **Website content**: the text in the message box, and the text you select when you use the right-click menu.

This data is processed only inside your browser to flag and replace personal details. It is never sent to Almma or anyone else.

## What PII Guard stores, and where

- **Placeholder key** (for example `[NAME_1]` = the original name): kept in the browser's session memory. It is never written to disk and is erased when the browser closes, or earlier if you press "Forget all placeholders now".
- **Settings, "always flag" list, "never flag" list:** kept in the browser's local extension storage on your computer. They are deleted when you remove the extension.

Web pages cannot read this data. It is not synced to any account.

## What PII Guard sends

Nothing. PII Guard contains no code that sends data over the network. It has no servers, analytics, telemetry, advertising or tracking. Neither Almma nor anyone else receives your text, your settings or your lists through PII Guard.

What you choose to send to an AI chat service after reviewing is sent by that website, under that website's own privacy policy.

## Permissions

- **Site access to claude.ai, chatgpt.com, gemini.google.com, copilot.cloud.microsoft and chat.almma.ai:** needed to check the message box on those five sites.
- **contextMenus:** adds the "always flag", "never flag" and "show originals" items to the right-click menu.
- **storage:** keeps your settings and lists on your computer, and the placeholder key in session memory.

## Children

PII Guard is not directed at children. It sends no data about anyone, including children, to Almma or anyone else.
