Quick setup

General

5 catches phone numbers, SSNs, ZIP codes and student IDs while leaving prices, scores and room numbers alone.

HIPAA identifiers

Rules for HIPAA identifiers that often appear in text, beyond names, phone numbers and e-mail. They do not cover every identifier on the HIPAA Safe Harbor list.

Always flag

One name or word per line, any capitalization. Right-click → "PII Guard: always flag" adds here.

Never flag

One per line. Exact matches are skipped, and these words are trimmed off the edges of names.

Your own patterns

A label (becomes the placeholder, e.g. COURSE → [COURSE_1]) and a regular expression. Example: label COURSE, pattern FIN\s?\d{3}.
